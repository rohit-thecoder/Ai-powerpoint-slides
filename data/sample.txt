MEETING NOTES - Q3 2026 BUSINESS REVIEW
Company: Northwind Analytics
Date: October 8, 2026
Attendees: Priya Sharma (CEO), Daniel Okafor (CFO), Mei Tanaka (VP Sales), Lucas Meyer (VP Product), Ana Costa (Head of Customer Success)

--------------------------------------------------
1. OPENING REMARKS
--------------------------------------------------
Priya opened by noting that Q3 was the strongest quarter in company history. Total revenue reached $4.74M, a 12% increase over Q2 and a 31% increase year over year. The leadership team agreed that the focus for Q4 should be profitable growth and expansion in Asia Pacific.

--------------------------------------------------
2. FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS (Daniel)
--------------------------------------------------
- Total Q3 revenue: $4.74M (Q2: $4.23M)
- Gross margin improved from 71% to 74%
- Operating expenses grew 6%, slower than revenue growth
- Cash runway extended to 28 months
- Annual recurring revenue (ARR) crossed $17M

--------------------------------------------------
3. SALES UPDATE (Mei)
--------------------------------------------------
- North America remains the largest market at 43% of revenue
- Asia Pacific is the fastest growing region at 18% quarter-over-quarter
- Won 3 enterprise deals above $250K each, including a major logistics company in Singapore
- Sales cycle for enterprise deals shortened from 94 days to 81 days
- Pipeline for Q4 stands at $6.2M with a 38% expected close rate

--------------------------------------------------
4. PRODUCT UPDATE (Lucas)
--------------------------------------------------
- Launched AI Assistant feature for Analytics Pro in July; adopted by 46% of active accounts within 10 weeks
- Insight Lite mobile app released in September with 12,000 downloads in the first month
- Planned for Q4: real-time dashboards, Slack and Teams integrations
- Top customer request: custom report templates (planned for Q1 2027)

--------------------------------------------------
5. CUSTOMER SUCCESS (Ana)
--------------------------------------------------
- Customer satisfaction score rose to 4.4 out of 5 (Q2: 4.2)
- Churn rate fell to 3.2%, the lowest recorded
- Net promoter score is 52, up 7 points
- Support response time averages 2.1 hours, down from 3.4 hours
- Main churn reason: pricing concerns among small business customers on Insight Lite

--------------------------------------------------
6. RISKS AND CHALLENGES
--------------------------------------------------
- Currency fluctuation may reduce European revenue by an estimated 2-3%
- Two competitors launched low-cost AI analytics tools in September
- Hiring for data science roles is taking 60+ days on average
- Dependence on a few large enterprise accounts (top 10 customers = 29% of revenue)

--------------------------------------------------

- Ana: Launch a pricing review for Insight Lite small business plan by November 30
- Daniel: Prepare a currency hedging proposal for the board
- Priya: Present Q3 results and Q4 plan to the board on October 20
